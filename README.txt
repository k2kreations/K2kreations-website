K2KREATIONS WEBSITE — FREE WEBSITE PACKAGE

FILES
- index.html: your responsive website
- logo.png: your supplied K2Kreations logo

DESIGN
Inspired by the reference storefront: black background, pink/purple gradient accents, bold navigation, large hero section, category panels, featured product cards, custom order form, newsletter-style strip, and dark footer. The product visuals are CSS mockups, not actual product photos.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Open index.html in a text editor.
2. Find the line: const BUSINESS_EMAIL = 'YOURBUSINESSEMAIL@example.com';
3. Replace the sample address with your real business email.
4. Save the file.

FREE PUBLISHING WITH GITHUB PAGES
1. Sign in or create a free account at https://github.com/
2. Create a PUBLIC repository named k2kreations-website.
3. Upload index.html and logo.png to the repository's main folder.
4. Open Settings > Pages.
5. Under Build and deployment, choose Deploy from a branch; choose main and /(root), then Save.
6. After GitHub publishes the site, it will show your website URL in the Pages section.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- The website does not process payments, store orders, or have a working shopping cart.
- The order form opens a prefilled email draft after you add your business email; the customer must send it.
- The newsletter sign-up is a visual demo and does not collect/store email addresses.
- Product cards use sample CSS illustrations. Replace them with your real product photos as you build your catalog.
